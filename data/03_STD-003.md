## S01. Classification approach

The data owner assigns the highest applicable class. Evaluate content, context and linkability rather than file extension or size. A summary, embedding or pilot copy does not automatically lower classification. When classification is uncertain, ask the data owner or Security before upload.

## S02. Public

Information formally approved for public release, such as published product guides and public blog posts. It may be processed only in an approved combination for its permitted purpose. Public status does not remove intellectual-property or accuracy-review responsibilities.

## S03. Internal

Routine nonpublic agendas, nonsensitive operating instructions and truly aggregate sales figures without customer-level records. Use company identities and deployments explicitly permitting Internal data. The source owner must confirm that aggregation removes identifiers and small-group reidentification risks.

## S04. Confidential nonpersonal

Nonpublic commercial plans, contracts without personal information, and source code without credentials, secrets or personal information. Only DEP-002 and DEP-003 permit specified Confidential nonpersonal uses; the approved purpose still applies. InsightDesk and Northstar Assist do not permit Confidential data.

## S05. Confidential personal

Customer names, email addresses, identifiable support transcripts and supplier contact details are Confidential personal data unless a higher class applies. All currently approved deployments exclude raw personal data. Privacy review alone does not grant permission where the catalog or policy still prohibits processing.

## S06. Restricted

Credentials, payment card details, production secrets, individual health records and individual HR performance records are Restricted. No registered deployment may process them. The exception route cannot override this rule. Stop upload and involve the data owner or Security.

## S07. Redaction and reassessment

Remove direct identifiers and indirect identifiers, unique account/order references, sensitive free text and details enabling reidentification. Reassess the remaining content with the owner. Routine meeting notes may become Internal only if no Confidential or Restricted information remains. Use synthetic examples when sanitization cannot be established.

## S08. RAG and derived artifacts

Source ACLs and classification apply to extracted text, chunks, embeddings, indexes, reranker inputs, retrieved context, evaluation datasets, traces and exports. Enforce authorization before retrieval and on parent expansion, evidence display and download. Do not expose excluded text to an external judge or reranker.

## S09. Regional scope

Catalog endpoints in this corpus are approved only for their recorded fictional region and environment. No blanket EU, Austria, APAC or cross-border permission is documented. Route regional questions to Privacy, Legal and Architecture; do not infer a legal conclusion.

## S10. Exposure response

If prohibited data was entered into a tool, stop further use, notify Security Incident intake, preserve relevant record identifiers and follow the incident owner instructions. Do not ask an assistant to repeat, recover or redistribute the exposed material.
