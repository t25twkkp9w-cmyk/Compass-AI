## S01. Screening outcomes

Low: assistance wholly inside an existing approved combination and purpose. Moderate: new internal custom applications or integrations without prohibited data. High: customer-facing generation or material consequential advice requiring accountable human oversight. Prohibited: any activity expressly barred by POL-001. These are fictional enterprise categories, not regulatory classifications.

## S02. Context and accountability

Record affected users, output use, business impact, data lineage, intended oversight and fallback. The assistant may suggest a tier, but Governance confirms screening. Consider the application behavior, not merely the model size or vendor reputation.

## S03. Customer-facing builds

Require sponsor endorsement and Technology, Security, Legal and Compliance/Risk reviews. Add Privacy when personal data is proposed. A missing Privacy assessment blocks a personal-data proposal, and a Privacy finding cannot override a catalog data restriction. Use AUTH-009 rules R02 and R04.

## S04. Internal custom builds

Require sponsor endorsement and Technology and Security reviews. Add Legal for contractual/IP interpretation and Privacy for proposed personal data. Routine existing tool use within scope is not a custom build. Use AUTH-009 rules R01 and R03.

## S05. Model and deployment changes

Evaluate generation, embedding and reranking versions separately. Changing the embedding model requires a separate consistent index and retrieval reevaluation; do not mix vector spaces. Model/provider/endpoint/region changes require a recorded assessment. See ENG-007 S06.

## S06. Conditions and residual risk

Describe compensating controls, owner, evidence, phase, due date and monitoring. A pre-release condition is not satisfied by promising to fix it later. Ongoing obligations remain tracked after clearance. A prohibited activity cannot be downgraded to Low through a pilot or exception.

## S07. Reassessment triggers

New users, data categories, system integration, autonomous behavior, material retrieval/prompt changes, security incidents and expiring authority trigger reassessment. The new snapshot must reference previous versions and identify which reviews remain applicable; affected reviews must be redone.

## S08. Worked screening examples

Public blog drafting in DEP-001: Low. New internal RAG application in proposed DEP-009: Moderate. Existing contract summary in DEP-003 within purpose: Low. Customer support RAG application: High. Confidential nonpersonal code in DEP-002 within purpose: Low. Autonomous hiring selection: Prohibited.
