## S01. Can AI draft a customer email?

Yes, within DEP-001 limits. Use Public or Internal nonpersonal inputs and have a competent employee review before sending.

Governing citations: POL-001@3.0:S02, POL-001@3.0:S06, REG-002@3.0:DEP-001.

## S02. Can I upload a spreadsheet with customer names and emails?

No currently approved deployment permits raw personal data. Removing the name column alone is not sufficient redaction.

Governing citations: STD-003@3.0:S05, STD-003@3.0:S07, REG-002@3.0:S01.

## S03. Can I summarize a nonpublic contract?

Use DEP-003 only for Confidential nonpersonal contract summarization. Remove personal data and secrets; Legal validates interpretations.

Governing citations: STD-003@3.0:S04, REG-002@3.0:DEP-003, POL-001@3.0:S06.

## S04. Can I paste an API key into CodeForge to debug an error?

No. Credentials are Restricted even when source code itself is permitted.

Governing citations: POL-001@3.0:S03, STD-003@3.0:S06, REG-002@3.0:DEP-002.

## S05. Which tool can analyze aggregate weekly sales?

InsightDesk DEP-004 can analyze Public or Internal aggregate data. Customer-level personal records are excluded.

Governing citations: REG-002@3.0:DEP-004, STD-003@3.0:S03, STD-003@3.0:S05.

## S06. Does a model approval authorize our chatbot?

No. A custom customer-facing application needs the exact deployment and case approval, required reviews and release evidence.

Governing citations: POL-001@3.0:S01, AUTH-009@3.0:S02, ENG-007@3.0:S05.

## S07. Can I use my personal account for routine company notes?

No. Company work requires the approved company account and deployment.

Governing citations: POL-001@3.0:S05.

## S08. Can a pilot bypass the approval rules?

No. Pilot-only status limits users and purpose; it does not authorize production, prohibited data or autonomous consequential decisions.

Governing citations: REG-002@3.0:S02, POL-001@3.0:S03, RSK-006@3.0:S06.

## S09. How do I get access to Northstar Assist?

Confirm DEP-001 scope, then send purpose, manager and data categories to IT Service Desk. Target is two business days after complete intake, not a guarantee.

Governing citations: PROC-004@3.0:S01, REG-002@3.0:DEP-001.

## S10. What if the requested tool is not in the register?

Do not infer approval. Prepare a new-tool/use-case intake and wait for the relevant assessment.

Governing citations: POL-001@3.0:S05, PROC-004@3.0:S02.

## S11. Can AI make a hiring shortlist from individual performance reviews?

Autonomous hiring decisions are prohibited and individual performance records are Restricted. A case exception cannot authorize the proposal.

Governing citations: POL-001@3.0:S03, STD-003@3.0:S06, AUTH-009@3.0:S05.

## S12. Can we change Vector Pine 1.0 to 1.1 without reapproval?

No. MOD-008@1.1 and DEP-011 are under review; use a separate index, impact assessment and regression evidence.

Governing citations: REG-002@3.0:MOD-008@1.1, REG-002@3.0:DEP-011, ENG-007@3.0:S06.

## S13. Who can give final approval?

The assigned authorized decision owner, after mandatory reviews. Sponsor, coordinator and administrator status alone are insufficient.

Governing citations: PROC-004@3.0:S05, AUTH-009@3.0:S08.

## S14. Can the person uploading evidence verify it too?

No. A distinct assigned independent verifier must inspect the exact artifact/configuration.

Governing citations: PROC-004@3.0:S07, AUTH-009@3.0:S08.

## S15. Does conditional approval mean ready for production?

No. All pre-release conditions must pass and release authority must record clearance. Ongoing obligations continue after release.

Governing citations: AUTH-009@3.0:S09, PROC-004@3.0:S07.

## S16. Can an approved model be used in a new overseas region?

No regional permission can be established from this corpus beyond US-SYN-1. Ask Privacy, Legal and Architecture; do not assume authorization.

Governing citations: STD-003@3.0:S09, REG-002@3.0:S01.

## S17. What if two policies conflict?

Identify the conflicting requirements, abstain from definitive permission and contact the source owners. FAQ examples cannot override authoritative rules.

Governing citations: POL-001@3.0:S09.

## S18. Can I keep using an application after approval expires?

No. Renew before expiration; stop scoped use after expiry until reauthorization.

Governing citations: PROC-004@3.0:S08, AUTH-009@3.0:S09.

## S19. Will a read-only source ACL still apply to embeddings?

Yes. Classification and source permissions apply to chunks, embeddings, retrieval context, reranker inputs and exports.

Governing citations: STD-003@3.0:S08, ENG-007@3.0:S01.

## S20. Can an LLM judge approve release evidence?

It can assist evaluation, but it cannot act as the authorized human verifier or decision owner. Synthetic fixtures are not proof of actual test execution.

Governing citations: AUTH-009@3.0:S08, ENG-007@3.0:S05.
