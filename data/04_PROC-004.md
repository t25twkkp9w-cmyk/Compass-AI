## S01. Approved-tool access

Submit tool/deployment ID, purpose, manager and intended data categories to IT Service Desk. Check the exact permitted combination first. Access target is two business days after a complete request; service targets are not guarantees or automatic approvals.

## S02. Use-case intake

Provide purpose, requester, sponsor, users, tools, all generation/embedding/reranking model versions, endpoints, hosting region, data classes/flows, oversight, impact and evaluation plan. Submit categories, not raw sensitive examples. Submission freezes a numbered snapshot.

## S03. Triage and information requests

Governance checks completeness, confirms the risk screening and applies AUTH-009. Triage target is three business days after complete intake. Missing information pauses triage or review; the coordinator records the requested fields and accountable owner. Do not treat an overdue target as approval.

## S04. Domain review and blockers

Assigned specialists record satisfactory, changes-requested or blocking findings for a specific request version. They cite requirements and evidence. A domain finding is not final disposition. Required unsatisfactory findings prevent approval; the decision owner cannot silently override them.

## S05. Formal decisions

Only the assigned authorized decision owner records Approved, Approved with conditions, Rejected or Return for revision. Record rationale, exact scope, applicable policy/routing versions, validity and conditions. Requesters may not decide their own cases. A sponsor or administrator has no implied decision authority.

## S06. Exceptions

Name the requirement, business need, compensating control, risk owner, duration and policy owner. Permitted exceptions last at most 90 days and need the decision owner plus affected policy-owner approval. Exceptions cannot permit Restricted data, personal accounts, autonomous prohibited decisions or raw personal data in a deployment that excludes it.

## S07. Evidence and release

Separate pre-release conditions from ongoing obligations. Evidence owners submit versioned artifacts; a distinct assigned verifier inspects them against the exact configuration. Only the release authority clears the case after all pre-release conditions pass. Ongoing monitoring remains required after release.

## S08. Change, renewal, expiry and revocation

Open a new request version for material scope changes. Reopen affected reviews. Renew before the valid-until timestamp; after expiry stop the scoped activity until reauthorization. Security or Governance may revoke an approval through a recorded decision; preserve original history.

## S09. Escalation and incidents

Policy ambiguity: AI Governance Operations. Data classification: data owner or Security. Proposed personal data: Privacy plus catalog-owner assessment. Suspected exposure: Security Incident intake immediately. All named teams and internal routes are fictional service records in this corpus.

## S10. Records, corrections and retention

Keep immutable submitted snapshots and append superseding decisions with reasons. Retain records for 24 months after closure in this fictional schedule, extended by legal hold. Restrict cases and exports by assignment. Record timestamps in UTC. History integrity requires server controls; synthetic hashes are demonstrations only.
