## S01. Scope and approval boundary

This policy covers employees, contractors and teams using AI for Northstar work. Permission is the intersection of activity, exact tool/model/deployment combination, purpose, data class, user population and approval conditions. Approval of an underlying model never authorizes an unlisted endpoint or a new customer-facing deployment. Use REG-002 for asset permissions and AUTH-009 for review authority.

## S02. Permitted everyday assistance

Within an existing approved combination, employees may draft, summarize, brainstorm, analyze permitted aggregate information and obtain code assistance. Routine use within recorded purpose and data limits needs tool access but no new use-case review. Review generated output before using it in a business process or communicating externally.

## S03. Absolute prohibitions

Do not use AI to make autonomous hiring, termination, credit or disciplinary decisions. Do not upload credentials, payment card details, production secrets, individual health records or individual HR performance records to any deployment in this corpus. No exception under PROC-004 may override these prohibitions.

## S04. Customer-facing and custom applications

New customer-facing generation, custom RAG applications, new system integrations and automated consequential advice require documented use-case review. A tool subscription, a pilot designation and sponsor endorsement do not grant production authorization. Release requires an applicable decision plus verified pre-release conditions.

## S05. Account and deployment requirements

Use company-managed identities, approved endpoints and required logging/retention settings. Personal consumer accounts are not permitted for company work. Do not copy an approved model name into an unapproved service and assume permission carries over. Unlisted tools have no documented approval.

## S06. Human accountability

The named employee remains responsible for accuracy, fairness and suitability. Customer communications require a competent reviewer. Generated code follows code review, testing and security checks. Legal interpretations require Legal review. The assistant provides guidance, not a formal approval or legal opinion.

## S07. Data handling

STD-003 determines classification and redaction. Raw personal data is excluded from every currently approved deployment in REG-002. Removing names alone does not establish anonymity. An approved Confidential nonpersonal use does not permit Confidential personal data.

## S08. Material change and renewal

A model-version change, new data class, region, provider, purpose, integration or audience requires change assessment under RSK-006. An earlier decision is scoped to its exact request version; a superseding version must not silently inherit affected reviews or release clearance. Expired, revoked and rejected decisions do not authorize continuing use.

## S09. Conflicts and missing evidence

Apply every relevant restriction. FAQ-005 is illustrative and cannot override authoritative rules. When authoritative sources conflict on the same requirement, identify the conflict, withhold definitive permission and contact the owners. Missing evidence, unknown regional approval and a low similarity score never imply permission.

## S10. Reporting and recordkeeping

Report suspected sensitive-data exposure immediately through Security Incident intake. Do not paste the exposed data into AI Compass. Record approval rationale, source versions and evidence references; log formal actions under AUD-012. Retain records under ENG-007 and PROC-004.
