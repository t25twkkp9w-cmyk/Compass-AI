## S01. RAG source controls

Use owner-approved sources with stable document/section/version IDs, effective dates, hashes and permissions. Preserve exclusions and cross-references during parsing. Maintain a source manifest and reject mixed or superseded current snapshots. Historical reconstruction uses the exact historical snapshot.

## S02. Retrieval design

For the AI Compass project compare dense, BM25 and hybrid retrieval. Initial candidate design is dense top 8 plus lexical top 8, RRF fusion, deduplication, optional reranking and parent/cross-reference expansion. These are project configuration choices, not mandatory settings for every enterprise application.

## S03. Evaluation metrics and targets

For the fictional AI Compass release, target required-section Recall@5 >=0.90, claim faithfulness >=0.95, citation precision >=0.95, correct abstention >=0.90 and p95 complete-response latency <=10 seconds. Also report false abstention, citation completeness, restriction coverage, usefulness and cost. Report counts and denominators; targets are not measured results.

## S04. Dataset and judge controls

Use 50 labeled questions: 15 direct, 10 multi-source, 10 missing-context, 10 unanswerable/conflict and 5 adversarial. Use 30 development and 20 held-out cases, keeping paraphrase families in one split. Gold labels must be reviewed. Calibrate judges against human samples and keep evaluator/model versions. Do not tune on the holdout.

## S05. Release evidence

Attach a configuration manifest, evaluated dataset version, raw outputs, metric numerators/denominators, failure disposition, access-control tests, incident/rollback plan and oversight ownership. A synthetic fixture is never proof that actual model or security tests ran. Required pre-release evidence must be independently verified for the exact request version.

## S06. Change validation

Changing generation, embedding or reranking models, prompts, chunking, corpus, permissions, hosting or integrations requires impact assessment and relevant regressions. Preserve old indexes until migration validation passes. A new model version does not inherit authorization automatically.

## S07. Monitoring and response

Track unsupported claims, prohibited guidance, missing evidence, stale sources, retrieval misses, latency and cost. Assign operations owners. A critical data-exposure signal triggers Security Incident intake and may revoke clearance. Never copy exposed payloads into monitoring or evaluation datasets unnecessarily.

## S08. Operational controls

Validate identity and case/document scope before retrieval, expansions, model calls and exports. Use parameterized queries and allowlisted tools. Validate state transitions server-side, apply idempotency keys and version checks, and append business events in the same transaction or through a transactional outbox.

## S09. Human oversight and retention

Define human handoff, release authority, rollback and manual fallback. In the fictional schedule retain case/evaluation evidence for 24 months after closure, longer under legal hold. LangSmith traces and application audit records serve different purposes; configure approved redaction and retention separately.
