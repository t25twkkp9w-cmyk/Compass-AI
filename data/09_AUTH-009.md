## S01. R01. Existing approved use

When activity, data, tool/model/deployment and users all match an existing approval: tool-access request only, no new use-case decision. Manager endorses access; IT provisions. Data exclusions and employee human review remain mandatory.

## S02. R02. Customer-facing custom build

Require sponsor endorsement and satisfactory Technology, Security, Legal and Compliance/Risk findings. Add Privacy under R04 when personal data is proposed. Assigned Governance Decision Owner records disposition; the same assigned owner clears release after required verification. No elapsed-time or majority-vote approval.

## S03. R03. Internal custom build

Require sponsor endorsement, Technology and Security. Add Legal for contractual/IP interpretation and Privacy under R04 for proposed personal data. Governance confirms route. Decision owner: assigned Governance Decision Owner.

## S04. R04. Personal-data proposal

Add Privacy to the applicable route. Current approved deployments exclude raw personal data; keep a blocking finding until a permitted alternative scope or a properly authorized policy/catalog change exists. A case-level exception alone cannot override the catalog restriction.

## S05. R05. Prohibited activity

Governance records rejection with POL-001 S03 or STD-003 S06. Do not collect unnecessary data or run a full approval sequence to authorize a prohibited use. Stop the scoped proposal; discuss a materially different permitted alternative if requested.

## S06. R06. Change and renewal

Use the base route for the changed scope and re-review affected domains. Preserve prior version decisions. A model or embedding upgrade reopens Technology, Security and Quality evidence assessment; customer-facing scope keeps Legal and Compliance/Risk requirements. Renewals need current evidence, not a copied old date.

## S07. R07. Permitted exception

Decision owner plus affected policy owner and all base required domain reviewers must approve a time-bound exception. Maximum 90 days. The requester may not be an approver. Absolute prohibitions remain in force.

## S08. R08. Separation of duties

Requester must differ from final decision owner. Evidence submitter must differ from independent verifier. Audit is read-only. A sponsor, coordinator or administrator has only assigned authority. The model cannot create its own authority or determine a final decision.

## S09. R09. Release and expiration

Only Approved or Approved with conditions disposition in its effective interval can support clearance. Every applicable pre-release condition must be verified. An expired or revoked disposition blocks clearance and continuing scoped use. Ongoing conditions remain monitored.

## S10. R10. Access and escalation

Apply case assignment and document scope independently. A domain reviewer does not get access to every company case. Coordinators manage missing information and escalate due dates. An escalation is not permission to skip a mandatory review.
